Therapists That Take EmblemHealth Insurance: How It Works at MMHC

EmblemHealth members, individuals and dependent family plan members, are eligible for psychotherapy services under their EmblemHealth plan. Depending on your plan, a copay, coinsurance, or deductible may apply; we confirm your exact cost before your first session.

Under the federal Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act, any limit EmblemHealth places on therapy visits can be no stricter than its limits on comparable medical care. If your coverage comes through a New York employer group plan, the state's Timothy's Law also guarantees at least 20 outpatient mental health visits per year. In practice, you can see an EmblemHealth therapist (one in-network with your EmblemHealth plan) weekly as long as your coverage is active and there is a medically necessary reason for your visits (medically necessary: care your plan considers required to treat a diagnosed condition). About 1 in 4 New York City adults experiences a mental health disorder in a given year, and outpatient therapy is the standard covered treatment for most of them.

About EmblemHealth

EmblemHealth is a New York health insurer formed by the combination of GHI (Group Health Incorporated) and HIP (Health Insurance Plan of Greater New York), and it operates both as its plan families today. When you search for a GHI therapist or a HIP therapist, you are searching for an EmblemHealth therapist. EmblemHealth's plans include GHI and HIP commercial coverage along with Medicare and Medicaid offerings, and the carrier's name is sometimes written as Emblem Health. Its behavioral health benefits are administered by Carelon Behavioral Health, so a Carelon-listed provider and an EmblemHealth-covered therapist are the same in-network care. MMHC treats New York members only.

Does EmblemHealth Cover Mental Health?

Yes. EmblemHealth covers mental health therapy for its members, and most of our EmblemHealth clients pay between $0 and $25 per session. Your exact number depends on which EmblemHealth plan you hold and where you are in your plan year. Three terms decide it: the copay (copay: the fixed fee you owe at each therapy visit), the deductible (deductible: what you would pay out of pocket before coverage starts), and coinsurance (coinsurance: the percentage of the session cost you pay after the deductible is met). We confirm all three for your plan before your first session, and you get the number in writing.

Why MMHC Is Different for EmblemHealth Members

The real barrier to starting therapy is usually the unknown cost, so we remove it first. Before your first session, a Therapy Coordinator calls EmblemHealth and confirms your exact copay, coinsurance, and deductible status, a check that usually takes about 5 minutes, and puts your estimated cost in writing. Every MMHC client has their exact cost confirmed before session one; the price you hear is the price you pay.

Fit drives outcomes. The largest meta-analysis of the therapeutic alliance reviewed 295 studies covering more than 30,000 patients (Flückiger et al., 2018). It found that the relationship between client and therapist is one of the strongest predictors of whether therapy works, online or in person. At MMHC, a clinician matches you based on your goals, personality, and clinical needs, and if the fit is wrong, you switch therapists without restarting intake.

You choose from a bench of 91+ therapists across the conditions and methods listed below, matched to your specific concern rather than to whoever has an opening. Your first session is often within 5 days of submitting the form.

Plus, for EmblemHealth members specifically:

  • No in-network wait queue. EmblemHealth clients are scheduled as quickly as self-pay clients; insurance never puts you in a slower line.
  • Outcome-tracked care. Progress is measured with the PHQ-9 for depression and the GAD-7 for anxiety, two validated symptom measures, so you and your therapist can see change instead of guessing at it.
